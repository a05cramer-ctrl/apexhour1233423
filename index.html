Rules

  • Hours are clock hours. Scores reset at :00.
  • Only trades inside the hour count. Any route counts: StonkFun curve, the pool after migration, aggregators.
  • To win you must be in profit and have traded at least that hour.
  • Prize = every creator fee the launch wallet received that hour. Ties go to whoever got there first.
  • No winner? The pot rolls into the next hour.
